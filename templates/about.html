About STEMPath ℹ️

Built to help students in grades 7–12 discover STEM careers with honest, transparent guidance.

Our Purpose 🎯

STEMPath helps middle and high school students (grades 7–12) explore Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics careers. We believe every student deserves a clear, encouraging picture of what a STEM career looks like—from the classes to take in ninth grade to the first job title to search for after graduation.

This tool is designed for students, counselors, and parents. There's no account required, no data collected, and no ads. Your choices are saved only in your browser's session.

How Recommendations Work 🔍

The recommendation logic is simple, transparent, and deterministic—it works the same way every time with the same inputs:

Score formula:
Raw Score = (1.0 × interest tag matches) + (1.5 × strength-derived tag matches)
Final Score = normalize Raw Score to 0–100
Ties = broken by number of matching technical skills
  1. Interest matching: Each career has topic tags (e.g., "coding," "biology"). When you select an interest, we count how many tags it shares with each career.
  2. Strength bonus: Strengths like "analytical thinking" map to interest tags like "math" and "data." Strength matches receive a 1.5× weight because they suggest a natural fit beyond just interest.
  3. Normalization: Scores are normalized so the best match always shows near 100%. This prevents a career from always looking low just because you selected few interests.
  4. No black boxes: The score is based entirely on the tags you see on each card. If a career has the same tags you selected, it scores high. Simple.

Why we keep it simple: We believe students should be able to understand why a career was recommended. A transparent formula is more trustworthy than a hidden algorithm, even if a fancier model might be more "accurate."

Privacy & Data 🔒

  • No account or login required.
  • Your quiz answers (interests, strengths) are stored only in your browser session—they are not sent to any server database.
  • No tracking, analytics, or advertising of any kind.
  • Closing your browser tab clears all saved data.

A note for students and families 💙

STEMPath is a starting point, not a final answer. Career paths are personal and shaped by many factors beyond any quiz. We strongly encourage you to discuss your interests and plans with a school counselor, trusted teacher, or family member before making any major decisions. This tool is here to spark curiosity and open doors— not to close them.
